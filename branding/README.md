# PDF branding assets

The Sitterwise logo is the company logo from https://sitterwise.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Logo.avif, converted to JPEG on white for the offline PDF renderer. It remains Sitterwise’s brand asset.

Poppins Regular, Semibold and Bold are the Google Fonts Latin TrueType subsets, distributed under the accompanying SIL Open Font License (Poppins-OFL.txt).

The PDF renderer uses ReportLab’s native JPEG support; Pillow is bundled as required by ReportLab.
